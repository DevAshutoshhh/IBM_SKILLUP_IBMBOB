# SaathiSetu — Three-Minute Demo Script

**Total runtime: 3:00.** Word counts assume a calm ~150 words per minute. Practise until you
land inside 2:55 — going over is worse than finishing early.

**Before you start:** clear the app's data (Privacy → Delete all my data) so the demo begins
from a genuinely blank state. Have the app open on the landing page.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · The problem

> **On screen:** the landing page, hero visible.

**Say:**

"Meet a student in Class 12. She's the first person in her family to get this far, and she
qualifies for at least three scholarships.

She won't get any of them.

Not because of her marks — because the information is on six different portals, the eligibility
rules are written in official language, and nobody told her she needed a domicile certificate
until the day the form was due.

This is the ordinary case, not the unlucky one. And the students who face the most process are
the ones with the least help at home."

*(~95 words · 20 seconds)*

---

## 0:20 – 0:40 · The solution

> **On screen:** scroll slowly through "How it works". Do **not** click yet.

**Say:**

"SaathiSetu is a bridge — *saathi*, companion; *setu*, bridge.

Four steps. Describe your situation. See matches that explain themselves. Get document-ready.
Print a plan you can carry.

And the whole thing runs in your browser. No account, no server, no Aadhaar, no uploads. Because
a student shouldn't have to hand over identity numbers just to find out whether an application is
worth starting."

*(~75 words · 20 seconds)*

---

## 0:40 – 1:20 · Profile and matching

> **On screen:** click **Find my opportunities**. Move briskly.

**Do and say:**

1. *(Step 1)* Select **Class 11 or 12**, then **Science**. → "Five short steps."
2. *(Step 2)* Choose **Bihar**. → "Where she studies."
3. *(Step 3)* Choose **₹1 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh** and **75% to 90%**.
   → "Notice: income as a *band*, not a figure. We never need the exact number, so we never ask
   for it."
4. *(Step 4)* **Pause here.** Point at "Prefer not to say".
   → "Every question on this step is optional — category, gender, disability. And choosing
   'Prefer not to say' will never remove an opportunity from her results. It's marked as
   something to verify. That one decision is why this tool is safe to use."
   Select **Prefer not to say** for social category.
5. *(Step 5)* Tick **Tuition assistance** and **Study materials**. Click **See my opportunities**.
6. Let the results land.
   → "Fourteen schemes, ranked by how much of the preparation she already has in place."

*(~150 words + interaction · 40 seconds)*

---

## 1:20 – 1:55 · Match explanation and comparison

> **On screen:** the results list.

**Do and say:**

1. On the top card, click **Why this match?**
   → "This is the part that matters. Not a number — a reason."
2. Point at each group in turn:
   → "Confirmed: her course level, her State, her income band inside the stated limit.
   To verify: she didn't state her category, so we tell her exactly what to check rather than
   silently dropping the scheme.
   Conflicts: anything that clearly rules her out — still shown, because rules change and the
   official portal is the authority."
3. Point at the note under the score.
   → "And we say it plainly: this percentage is a preparation aid. It is not an eligibility
   decision."
4. Click **Add to comparison** on two or three cards, then **Compare** in the navigation.
   → "Three at a time, side by side — match, income condition, documents required, readiness,
   deadline. On a phone this becomes stacked cards, not a table you have to scroll sideways to
   read."

*(~135 words + interaction · 35 seconds)*

---

## 1:55 – 2:30 · Document readiness and action plan

> **On screen:** go back to **Opportunities**, click **Prepare this application** on the top card.

**Do and say:**

1. → "Here's the real bottleneck. Discovery is the visible problem. *Documents* are what
   actually stops applications."
2. Mark two documents **Ready**, and mark the **income certificate** as **Needs renewal**.
   → "Four statuses — and 'Needs renewal' is its own state, deliberately. An income certificate
   that expired last month is one of the most common reasons an application fails, and one of the
   most preventable."
3. Point at the ring and the next-actions list.
   → "A readiness score, and a prioritised list: missing documents before expiring ones. She
   always knows the single next thing to do. And every document says *why* it's asked for."
4. → "Nothing is uploaded. There is no file input in this application at all. It's a checklist."
5. Click **Build my action plan**. Scroll it once.
   → "And this is what she carries away: her scores, the reasons, what to verify before applying,
   documents in order, next steps, the official link, and a plain warning that the portal is the
   authority."
6. Click **Print or save as PDF**; show the print preview; close it.
   → "With a real print stylesheet. This goes to a cyber cafe, a school office, or a parent."

*(~185 words + interaction · 35 seconds)*

---

## 2:30 – 2:50 · Privacy, Hindi, accessibility and offline

> **Move fast here — four proof points, five seconds each.**

**Do and say:**

1. Click **हिंदी** in the header.
   → "Full Hindi — navigation, forms, validation, and the match explanations themselves."
   Switch back to English.
2. Click **Privacy**.
   → "Exactly what's stored, where it's stored — one key in localStorage — and one button that
   deletes all of it. Never collected: name, Aadhaar, phone, address, bank details, documents."
3. Tab through the header two or three times so the focus ring is clearly visible.
   → "Fully keyboard operable, visible focus, labelled controls, and status never signalled by
   colour alone."
4. Open DevTools → Network → **Offline**, and reload.
   → "And it's an installable PWA. Offline, the entire workflow still works — profile, matching,
   checklist, plan. Only the links out to official portals need a connection. Which is the
   connection our student actually has."

*(~140 words + interaction · 20 seconds)*

---

## 2:50 – 3:00 · Closing impact

> **On screen:** return to the action plan, or the landing page.

**Say:**

"A student who finds out in September that she needs a domicile certificate has three months to
get one. A student who finds out at submission has none.

SaathiSetu is that three months. No server, no cost, works offline, and it never asks who you
are.

That's the bridge."

*(~65 words · 10 seconds)*

## Recording checklist

**Before recording**

- [ ] `npm run build && npm run preview` — demo the production build, not the dev server.
- [ ] Privacy → **Delete all my data**, so the demo starts blank.
- [ ] Language set to **English**.
- [ ] Browser zoom at **100%**; window at **1440×900** or 1080p.
- [ ] Hide bookmarks bar, extensions, and any personal tabs.
- [ ] Close notifications, chat apps and email. Enable Do Not Disturb.
- [ ] Increase cursor size or enable click highlighting.
- [ ] Test microphone levels; record in the quietest room you have.
- [ ] Have DevTools ready but **closed**, docked to the right, for the offline demo.
- [ ] Do one full silent run-through to get the click path into your fingers.

**While recording**

- [ ] Start with the app already loaded — never record a page load.
- [ ] Speak slower than feels natural; move the mouse deliberately.
- [ ] Pause for a beat after clicking, before speaking, so the screen catches up.
- [ ] Do not read the interface aloud — say what it *means*.
- [ ] If you fumble, keep going. Fix it in the edit.
- [ ] Hit the 0:40 / 1:20 / 1:55 / 2:30 marks; if you are behind, trim the comparison step.

**Must appear on screen**

- [ ] Hero heading: "Turn eligibility confusion into a clear action plan"
- [ ] A "Prefer not to say" option, explicitly pointed out
- [ ] An expanded match explanation showing all three groups
- [ ] The comparison view
- [ ] A document marked "Needs renewal" and the readiness ring reacting
- [ ] The printable action plan and its print preview
- [ ] The Hindi interface
- [ ] The privacy panel
- [ ] A visible keyboard focus ring
- [ ] The app working with the network offline

**Must be said out loud**

- [ ] "This is a prototype with demonstration data."
- [ ] "The percentage is a preparation aid, not an eligibility decision."
- [ ] "Choosing 'Prefer not to say' never removes an opportunity."
- [ ] "Nothing is uploaded; nothing leaves the browser."
- [ ] "The official portal remains the authority."

**After recording**

- [ ] Trim dead air at the start and end.
- [ ] Confirm the final cut is **under 3:00**.
- [ ] Add captions — a judge may watch with the sound off.
- [ ] Check that no personal data, bookmark or email address is visible in any frame.
- [ ] Export at 1080p and watch the whole thing once before submitting.
